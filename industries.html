Defense & Aerospace

Aerospace & Defense

Aerospace components demand dimensional precision, material consistency, and traceability that goes beyond most industries. BrokerMFG produces lightweight structural and functional plastic parts for aircraft interiors, avionics systems, ground support equipment, and defense programs.

Our quality documentation — including material certs, first article inspections, and process records — supports the traceability requirements typical in aerospace supply chains.

Avionics HousingsInterior PanelsBrackets & ClipsConnector BodiesSensor HousingsCable Guides

Key Requirements We Meet

Tight tolerances — ±0.001" and tighter achievable with proper tool design and material selection

Material documentation — full material certs and traceability on every lot

Lightweight engineering resins — including flame-retardant grades where required

FAI & PPAP — first article inspection reports provided as standard
