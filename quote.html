Quick Quote FAQ

What if I don't have a CAD file yet?
No problem. You can describe your part in the form or attach a 2D sketch, hand drawing, or photo of a sample. Our engineers will follow up with questions and can help develop a 3D model as part of our design engineering service.
Is the quote really free with no commitment?
Yes — completely free, no commitment required. We provide a detailed quote including DFM feedback because we believe in showing our value upfront. You decide whether to move forward.
How do you keep my design confidential?
All submitted files and project information are treated as strictly confidential. We are happy to sign a mutual NDA before you share sensitive design files — just request one when you submit the form or call us.
What is DFM and why does it matter?
DFM (Design for Manufacturability) is an engineering review of your part to identify features that could cause molding problems or increase cost — such as inadequate draft angles, thin walls, or features requiring complex side-actions. Addressing these before tooling can save thousands of dollars and weeks of delay.
