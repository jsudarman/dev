About BrokerMFG

Over 40 years of precision thermoplastic injection molding — from a single press to a full-service manufacturing partner for manufacturers across North America.

Our Story

Built on Precision. Grown on Trust.

Broker Manufacturing LLC was founded in 1984 in the greater Milwaukee area with a simple mission: deliver plastic injection molded parts that are right the first time, every time.

Over four decades, we've grown from a small shop into a full-service custom injection molding operation — adding engineering capabilities, an in-house toolroom, and modern Toyo injection presses ranging from 28 to 300 tons.

Through it all, we've maintained what made us successful from day one: hands-on ownership, close customer relationships, and an unwavering commitment to quality. We don't just mold plastic — we solve manufacturing problems for engineers and procurement teams who need a reliable partner they can count on.

Our History

40+ Years of Growth & Innovation

From a single-press shop to a full-capability custom molding operation — here's how BrokerMFG got to where we are today.

1984
BrokerMFG Founded
Broker Manufacturing LLC established in the greater Milwaukee area, focused on custom thermoplastic injection molding for local industrial customers.
Early 1990s
Toolroom Expansion
Added in-house mold design and fabrication capabilities, allowing us to offer complete design-to-production services under one roof.
Late 1990s
Aerospace & Industrial Growth
Expanded into aerospace and precision industrial markets, investing in tighter-tolerance capabilities and quality systems to meet demanding customer requirements.
2000s
Modern Toyo Press Fleet
Upgraded to an all-Toyo injection molding press fleet ranging from 28 to 300 tons, delivering greater precision, repeatability, and energy efficiency.
2010s
CAD & Mold Flow Analysis
Invested in CAD engineering capabilities and mold flow simulation software, enabling Design for Manufacturability reviews before tooling begins.
2024–2026
Digital Transformation & Growth
Modernizing customer experience with online quoting, expanded material offerings, and enhanced quality documentation — while staying true to our roots of personal service and engineering excellence.
